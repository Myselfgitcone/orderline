Will this order arrive late?

A shop's Postgres database, captured change by change with Debezium, rebuilt in Databricks, and scored at checkout by a model that has to get the real causes of lateness right, not just the score.

What makes an order late

The last 30 days. Checkout promises 5 days for standard and 2 for express, whoever carries it and wherever it ships from. These are why that promise breaks.

By carrier

Late share of delivered orders.

By warehouse

Load is orders in the previous 24 hours over daily capacity. Overflow is orders sent from a warehouse that isn't the customer's nearest.

The model

Gradient-boosted trees on what's known at checkout, trained on older weeks and graded on the latest two.

Does it find the real causes?

The simulator planted them, so each is checked: same orders, one thing changed, average predicted risk.

    What it leans on

    How much accuracy (AUC) drops when each input is shuffled.

    Track record

    Open orders are scored after each sync while they can still make their promise date. Once that date passes, the first score each one got is compared with what actually happened.
